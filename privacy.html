Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026.

This OAuth client is used only by its owner, with the open-source tool rclone, to copy the owner's own files to the owner's own Google Drive.

What data is accessed

With the owner's permission, rclone reads and writes files in the owner's Google Drive in order to upload and verify backups.

How data is used and stored

Data moves directly between the owner's computer and Google Drive. The access token is stored only on the owner's computer, in the rclone configuration file.

Sharing

No data is collected by, sent to, or shared with the developer or any third party. There are no servers, analytics or advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: github.com/maxcamov1-max/rclone-app/issues.
